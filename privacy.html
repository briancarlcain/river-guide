River Guide — Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

River Guide is a trip-planning app for river trips. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: your trips stay on your device unless you choose to share one with your crew, and we collect nothing else.

What we collect

River Guide has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not receive your trips, names, notes, costs or anything else you enter, with one exception you control: if you tap Share with crew on a trip, a copy of that trip (its crew names, itinerary, meals, gear, costs and notes) is stored on a server so your crew's devices can stay in sync. Only that trip is sent, and only after you choose to share it.

Shared trips

A shared trip is stored under a random 16-character join code. We store only a one-way hash of the code, not the code itself. Anyone who has the code can view and edit the trip, so share it only with people you trust. The server keeps no names, emails, device identifiers or location. Choosing Stop sharing in the app deletes the server copy immediately; people who already synced keep the copy on their own device. Crew members can Leave shared trip at any time. Server hosting is provided by Supabase; the usual short-lived network and security logs kept by a hosting provider are outside our control.

Where your data lives

Trips and rivers you create are stored locally on your device. If you delete the app, or choose Erase all data in the app, the local copy is removed (a trip you shared stays on the server until its owner chooses Stop sharing). We cannot recover local data for you, so use General → About → Back up all trips to save a copy.

Backups and sharing

When you back up your trips or share a trip summary, the app hands the file or text to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes (Files, iCloud Drive, Messages, Mail and so on) is your choice and is governed by that service's own policies.

Notifications

If you turn on trip reminders, the app asks permission and schedules local notifications on your device before your launch date. They are generated on the device; no notification service or server is involved. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software. It contacts our sync server only for trips you have shared or joined. Links to this policy and to our support page open in your web browser.

Children

River Guide is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Safety information

Medical, rescue and river information in the app is general reference material and may be out of date or incomplete. It is not medical advice. Always confirm regulations, permits, flows and conditions with the managing agency, and carry appropriate emergency communication.
