River Guide — Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

River Guide is a trip-planning app for river trips. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: your information stays on your device, and we do not collect it.

What we collect

Nothing. River Guide has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not receive the trips, names, notes, costs or any other information you enter.

Where your data lives

Trips and rivers you create are stored locally on your device. If you delete the app, or choose Erase all data in the app, that information is removed. We cannot recover it for you, so use General → About → Back up all trips to save a copy.

Backups and sharing

When you back up your trips or share a trip summary, the app hands the file or text to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes (Files, iCloud Drive, Messages, Mail and so on) is your choice and is governed by that service's own policies.

Notifications

If you turn on trip reminders, the app asks permission and schedules local notifications on your device before your launch date. They are generated on the device; no notification service or server is involved. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software and makes no network requests of its own. Links to this policy and to our support page open in your web browser.

Children

River Guide is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Safety information

Medical, rescue and river information in the app is general reference material and may be out of date or incomplete. It is not medical advice. Always confirm regulations, permits, flows and conditions with the managing agency, and carry appropriate emergency communication.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
